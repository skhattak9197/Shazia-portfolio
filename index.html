Microsoft Entra ID · Practice lab

A role changed. Did the access change too?

Situation

In a practice tenant, John moved from IT Support to Payroll.

Action

I removed his IT Support group membership and added him to the Payroll security group.

Verification

I checked group memberships and explored audit events for membership changes.

Learning

A mover needs both old access reviewed and appropriate new access assigned.
